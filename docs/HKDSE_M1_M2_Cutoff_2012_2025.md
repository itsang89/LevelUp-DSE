## 📊 Quick Trend Notes

- **M1 5\*\*** cut-offs have ranged between **84–94%** (2012–2025), with 2021 being the highest (94%).
- **M2 5\*\*** cut-offs have been more stable, ranging **81–92%**, with 2015 being the highest (92%).
- **M2 is generally harder** to score high percentages in than M1, as reflected by lower cut-offs across all levels.
- Both subjects saw relatively **lower cut-offs in 2022–2023**, suggesting harder papers in those years.
